# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows - Power BI Interactive Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing Netflix's global content library. This interactive Power BI dashboard extracts key business insights regarding content distribution, genres, and release trends over time.

## 📌 Project Overview
Netflix continues to expand its international presence and content diversity. This project aims to analyze the dataset to understand:
- The proportion of **Movies vs. TV Shows** in Netflix's catalog.
- The **exact number of producing countries** involved in content creation.
- Distribution across target audience ratings (**Adults, Kids & Family, Teens**).
- Top performing content genres and release trends by year.

---

## 🛠️ Data Processing & Transformation
- **Data Cleaning (Google Sheets & Power Query):** Handled missing and blank records in `Country`, `Director`, and `Cast` fields.
- **Audience Grouping:** Categorized content ratings into clear audience groups (`Adults`, `Kids & Family`, `Teens`).
- **Country Disaggregation:** Resolved aggregated comma-separated values to count true distinct producing countries accurately.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Summary Metrics
- **Total Titles:** ~7.79K Titles
- **Movies vs. TV Shows:** Movies constitute **69%** (~5.38K) while TV Shows account for **31%** (~2.41K) of total content.
- **Geographic Reach:** Content spans across **122 distinct countries** worldwide.
- **Average Movie Duration:** ~99.32 Minutes.
- **Top Genre:** Documentaries and Stand-Up Comedy lead the individual category counts.
